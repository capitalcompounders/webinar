  • think in years and decades, not days

Who's teaching

I'm Rajive, a lifelong student of value investing. I've spent over 10 years learning this approach the slow way: reading, applying, making mistakes and writing down what they taught me.

I teach because I remember being a few steps back on this path and wishing someone had shown me the process instead of the hype. What I share is my own experience. Your results will depend on your own choices and circumstances.
